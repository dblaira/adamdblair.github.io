## 5. CODE THE PATTERN — the system

**Offer:** a fixed-scope, fixed-price productized service.

> **"Your agent ignores your rules. I'll fix that."**
> Send your repo + the rules your coding agent keeps breaking. You get back a
> constraint layer — rules written as enforceable checks, not suggestions —
> plus the config wiring so the agent loads them every session. $29. 48 hours.

Why this and not a template:
- Zero marginal cost; I do the work.
- Not competing on commodity documentation — competing on *enforcement*, which
  is the differentiated thing Adam actually has.
- **It compounds (conn-019).** Every delivery hardens a reusable template. After
  ~3 deliveries the service becomes a self-serve product with proof attached.
  The service buys the evidence the product needs.

**Storefront:** `adamdblair.github.io` — free, already owned, no platform cut.

**Rail:** Stripe Payment Link → the $29 charge. No code.
